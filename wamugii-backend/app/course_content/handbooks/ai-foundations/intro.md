Welcome to **AI Foundations**. This handbook contains the complete course in one book. It is written so that you can **learn everything by reading alone**: you do not need to watch the videos to understand it, although the online lessons, videos and quizzes are there to help you go further.

Everything is explained in **simple English**, step by step, with examples from Rwanda and everyday life. No coding is needed.

### How the handbook is organised

The course has **four weeks**, and each week has **six chapters**: five teaching chapters and one project chapter.

- **Week 1 · What AI Is:** definitions, history, agents, problem types and search.
- **Week 2 · How Machines Learn:** data, the machine-learning workflow, and supervised, unsupervised and reinforcement learning.
- **Week 3 · Measuring and Building Models:** training and testing, performance measures, neural networks and language models.
- **Week 4 · AI in Society:** fairness, explainability, privacy and security, governance and careers.

### What you will find in every chapter

- **In this chapter you will learn to:** the goals of the chapter. Read them first.
- **Numbered sections** (for example 5.2) that explain the ideas one step at a time.
- **Coloured boxes:**
  - **Key idea:** the most important point of a section. If you remember only one thing, remember this.
  - **Worked example:** a full example with every step shown. Work through it with a pen and paper.
  - **Common mistake:** a mistake many learners make, and how to avoid it.
  - **In Rwanda:** how the idea applies here.
- **Figures** that show the ideas as pictures.
- **Summary:** the chapter in a few lines. Use it to revise.
- **Key terms:** the new words, with short definitions. All of them are also in the **Glossary** at the back.
- **Self-check:** multiple-choice questions, the same as the online lesson quiz.
- **Think deeper:** open questions that make you apply the ideas. Write your own answer first.
- **Practice:** a short activity. Doing it is how the knowledge becomes yours.

The **Answer key** at the back gives the correct answer and the reason for every self-check question, and a model answer for every Think deeper question. A model answer shows **one** good answer; yours can use different words or examples and still be right.

### A good way to study each chapter

1. **Read the goals** at the start.
2. **Read the chapter slowly.** Do every worked example yourself before reading the result.
3. **Read the summary** and check that you can explain each line in your own words.
4. **Do the self-check** without looking back, then check your answers in the Answer key. Re-read any section where you made a mistake.
5. **Answer the Think deeper questions** in writing, then compare with the model answers.
6. **Do the practice activity.** Keep your work: the four project chapters (6, 12, 18 and 24) build on each other and become your final project.

Plan about **one chapter per day**, five days a week, with the project chapter at the end of each week. Studying a little every day works much better than reading many chapters at once.
